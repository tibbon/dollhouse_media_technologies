Installation · Burning Man 2019

Windchest

An interactive sculpture that recalls a pipe organ: softly lit columns rising out of the desert, ringed by LED-lit pads that compress like bellows. Each pad triggers a sound, so anywhere from one to fifteen people jumping on them play a single open-ended piece of music together.

I did the LEDs, audio, electrical, and programming. Sculpture by verbstudio, music by James Wright Glasgow, built with a crew of Boston fabricators, architects, and engineers.
