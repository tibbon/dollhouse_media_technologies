Installation · Burning Man 2019

Windchest

A sculpture like a pipe organ: lit columns ringed by LED pads that compress like bellows, so up to fifteen people jumping on them play one piece of music together.

I did the LEDs, audio, electrical, and programming. Sculpture by verbstudio, music by James Wright Glasgow.
