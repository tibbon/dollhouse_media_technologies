Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

Overview

BlackBox Audio Recorder (“BlackBox”) is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your audio recordings stay entirely on your Mac. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. BlackBox does not include analytics, telemetry, crash reporters, or any network communication. The app operates entirely offline.

Microphone Access

BlackBox requests access to your microphone solely to record audio to local WAV files on your Mac. Audio data is written directly to your chosen output directory and is never transmitted over the network.

Local Storage

BlackBox stores the following data locally on your Mac:

  • Audio recordings – WAV files saved to your chosen output directory
  • Preferences – Your settings (input device, output mode, bit depth, etc.) stored in macOS UserDefaults
  • Security-scoped bookmarks – A reference to your chosen output directory so the app can access it across launches

No data is stored on external servers or in the cloud.

Third-Party Services

BlackBox does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or frameworks that collect user data.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page with the new effective date.
