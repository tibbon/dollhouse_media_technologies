Release Notes

Every version of TenTen, newest first. Updates are free within your major version: download the latest from the links in your purchase email, or get them again. Your sessions and presets carry over.

1.3.2

2026-11-16

  • Undo and Redo, next to the page tabs: every knob turn, switch and preset load is a step. Cmd/Ctrl+Z works too when the plugin window has keyboard focus.
  • A/B compare: flip between two settings, or copy one to the other. Both are saved with your session.
  • The factory presets are grouped into submenus: Classics, Doubling & Width, Intervals & Stacks, Feedback, Played & Moving, and Broken & Bent. Your host's program list keeps its order, so sessions open as before.
  • The About box has the support email, release notes and website, and says whether you have the latest version.

1.2.2

2026-11-03

  • TenTen now runs on Linux: VST3, CLAP and a standalone app for 64-bit Linux, plus the free demo.
  • The Save preset box is ready to type in straight away, in every plugin format.
  • A name error clears as soon as you start typing.

1.2.1

2026-11-03

  • The scroll wheel now scrolls the lower pages when the pointer is over a knob, instead of changing that knob. Knobs on the front panel still respond to the wheel.

1.2.0

2026-11-02

  • Save your own presets from the preset menu, and delete them again. They're plain files that work in every plugin format and on every platform, so you can copy them between machines or share them.
  • Sessions remember which of your presets was selected.

1.1.3

2026-11-02

  • Picking the preset that's already showing now reloads it, so it clears your edits.

1.1.2

2026-11-02

  • Fixed a possible crash if a host saved the plugin's state while a session was loading.
  • A saved value that was out of range or unreadable is now saved back as the value the plugin actually used.

1.1.1

2026-11-02

  • Slightly lower CPU use, about 8% on the Init preset. No changes to the sound.

1.1.0

2026-11-02

  • Updated JUCE to 9.0.5. No changes to the sound.
  • Sessions now remember which preset was selected, so the preset menu shows the right name when you reopen one. The sound of a saved session was already restored correctly.
  • Saving a session you've just opened now gives back exactly the same data, so hosts that compare states won't see a change that isn't there.

1.0.0

2026-11-02

  • Version 1.0. No changes to the sound or features since 0.1.10; sessions and presets carry over.

0.1.10

0.1.9

0.1.0

2026-11-01

First release.

  • A pitch shifter and delay modelled on a 1970s glitchy pitch-shifting delay rack, with its converter, memory, crossfades, drift, aging and part tolerance all under your control.
  • Two complete units with cross-feedback, a second output, and linked multichannel use with Spread.
  • Manual, anti-feedback, MIDI keyboard and CV pitch control, with an envelope follower.
  • Bends, breakdowns and a Stock switch.
  • 41 factory presets, and a tooltip on every control.
  • A notice when a newer version is available, which you can turn off. It sends no telemetry.
  • VST3, Audio Unit, CLAP and a standalone app for macOS (signed and notarized). Pro Tools / AAX and Windows 11 coming soon.
