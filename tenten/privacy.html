Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026. This covers DMT TenTen and its purchase and download pages, run by Dollhouse Media Technologies, LLC, Providence, Rhode Island.

The plugin

TenTen has no telemetry, analytics or accounts, and it sends nothing about you or your music. It can read a public version file on this website at most once a day, to tell you when a new version is out. That is an ordinary web request, so the website’s host sees your IP address as it would for any page. You can turn this off in the plugin’s About panel.

When you buy

Payment is handled by Stripe and Link, who act as merchant of record under their own privacy policies (Stripe, Link). We never see your full card details.

We keep your email address, order number, amount, currency, purchase date and refund status, so we can email your download links, send new ones when you ask, honour refunds and keep business records. Our store runs on Amazon Web Services in the United States, which also sends those emails.

We don’t sell your information or use it for marketing.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep purchase records for as long as you may need your downloads and as our legal and tax records require. To see, correct or delete your information, email support@dollhousemediatech.com. Deleting your record means we can no longer re-send your download links.

This website

This website has no analytics or advertising and is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps ordinary server logs. Our download pages pass a private link token to our store; it stays out of the website’s logs.
