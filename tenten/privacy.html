TenTen Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Dollhouse Media Technologies, LLC ("we", "us"), a Rhode Island limited liability company, collects when you buy or download TenTen, use it, or contact us, and what we do with it. We are responsible (the "controller") for the data our own store and support inbox keep. Effective 2 November 2026.

In short

  • The TenTen plugin does not collect or send personal data. It has no telemetry or accounts.
  • Your payment is handled by Stripe's seller of record ("Link" on your receipt), not by us. We never see your card details.
  • To deliver your download, our store keeps your email address and order details.
  • We use this only to deliver and re-send your download, honor refunds, answer you and keep business records. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or send marketing.

What we collect

We collect only what buying, downloading and support need.

We do not record the IP addresses that open your download links, we do not count your downloads, and our pages use no analytics or advertising cookies. Your IP address still reaches our providers, as with any website: AWS when you open a download link or ask for a new one, and GitHub when you visit a page on this site. They may keep it in their own operational and security logs, under their own terms; we have not turned on any AWS logging that would record it for us. The plugin can read a public version file on our website at most once a day to tell you about updates. That sends no account or device identifiers, GitHub sees your IP address in the same way, and you can turn this off in the plugin's About panel.

How we use it, and our legal basis

For buyers in the EU and UK, the law asks us to name a legal basis for each use.

  • To deliver what you bought (sending and re-sending your download, refunds): performance of our contract with you.
  • To protect the store (limiting how often links can be re-sent, keeping links from working after a refund, error logs): our legitimate interest in keeping the service working.
  • To answer your messages: performance of our contract, or our legitimate interest in helping you.
  • To keep business and tax records: legal obligation.

We do not sell personal data, share it for targeted advertising, or make automated decisions about you.

Who else handles your data

  • Stripe and Link. Stripe's seller of record sells TenTen to you, takes payment, and handles tax and refunds requested through Link. It is responsible for that data under its own privacy policies (Stripe, Link), not this one. It sends us your email address and order details so we can deliver your download.
  • Our service providers, who process data only on our instructions: Amazon Web Services, which runs our store, holds purchase records and download files, sends download emails through Amazon SES, and receives your IP address when you use a download link, in its US East (Northern Virginia) region; and Google Workspace, which hosts our support email. When you ask for a new link, we look up your email address in our records on AWS.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website on GitHub Pages and sees your IP address when you visit, under its own privacy terms. Your private download token stays in your browser and is not sent to GitHub.
  • Others, only when needed: if the law or a valid legal request requires it, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or to a buyer of the business, who would remain bound by this policy.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or give you a copy. We answer within 30 days. To protect you, we may ask you to write from the email address you bought with.

  • EU and UK: you also have the right to object to our use based on legitimate interests, to restrict our use, and to complain to your data protection authority.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California): you can ask what we collect and why, and ask us to correct or delete it. We do not sell or share personal data as those laws define it, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To use any of these rights, email support@dollhousemediatech.com.

Other terms

  • Where data is stored. Our store and providers are in the United States. If you buy from outside the US, your data is transferred there so we can deliver your purchase.
  • Security. Download links expire and stop working after a refund, requests for new links are rate-limited and only ever emailed to the purchase address, access to our systems is restricted to us, and our stored data is encrypted at rest.
  • Children. TenTen is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (under 16 in the EU and UK).
  • Changes. If we change this policy we will post the new version with a new effective date, and email buyers about significant changes.
